# Daylight

A dedicated iPhone camera for sunrise and sunset sequences. Captures are saved as reversible edits in Apple Photos, and publishing adapters can deliver the selected highlight to social destinations. The first destination is Mastodon.

The phone stays on power with Daylight open and unlocked. iOS interrupts background camera use. Relaunch the app after a reboot or termination. Capture intervals are opportunities, not guarantees: interrupted slots are recorded as missed, never replayed as a burst.

The default is one photo every five minutes from 30 minutes before to 30 minutes after each solar event. Times use the offline NOAA fractional-year approximation and a configured location/time zone; terrain, window direction, and local weather are not predicted.
