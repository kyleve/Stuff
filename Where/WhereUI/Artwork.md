# Halloween artwork

The Halloween theme uses three original illustrations generated for Where with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-10-29.

- `HalloweenPumpkins.png`: apricot and cream pumpkins, sage vines, and lavender mushrooms.
- `HalloweenMoon.png`: a crescent moon, bat, and sparse stars.
- `HalloweenVine.png`: a sage vine with an apricot flower and lavender star.

Each PNG has a transparent background. The images contain no text or controls. Their shared palette uses cream, lavender, sage, apricot, and plum outlines.

`Sources/Resources/Themes/HalloweenTheme.json` owns placement, opacity, paint bounds, and motion. `HalloweenThemeAssets` supplies only these approved bundled resources. Native SwiftUI owns text, interaction, borders, and layout.
